📋 Product Management Architecture Rules
Stock Adjustment Safety
Direct quantity adjustments calculate expected total weight and verify that the 10kg load cell capacity limit is never exceeded.
RFID Uniqueness Check
Every RFID tag UID must be unique. Updating or pairing a tag checks for collisions across all registered products.
In-Memory DS Synchronization
Adding, updating, or deleting products automatically rebuilds the $O(1)$ Hash Table and Min-Heap Priority Queue.
Referential Deletion Protection
Products with recorded transaction history or existing physical stock cannot be deleted, preserving audit integrity.
