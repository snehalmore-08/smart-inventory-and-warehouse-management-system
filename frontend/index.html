🛡️ Physical Load Cell Verification Rules
  • Nominal Weight Check: Incoming sample weight must be within allowable tolerance (typically ±35g of ~500g).
  • 10kg Overload Guard: The system automatically verifies that Current Load + Added Weight ≤ 10,000g before executing the SQL transaction.
  • Atomic COMMIT: If the scale check passes, product stock quantity and total weight are updated atomically, and an immutable record is saved in inventory_transactions.

🛡️ Safe Stock Deduction & Verification
  • Non-Negative Stock Guarantee: Requests to stock out items with 0 available units or quantity exceeding current stock are actively rejected with HTTP 400 Bad Request.
  • Platform Weight Recalculation: Removing items recalculates products.current_total_weight and updates warehouse platform load.
  • Audit Logging: Every removal creates an immutable entry in inventory_transactions with before/after units and weight deltas.

4. MPCA: HX711 24-Bit ADC Two's Complement Decoder Computer Architecture

The HX711 analog-to-digital converter outputs a 24-bit signed integer. Bit 23 is the MSB (sign bit). This utility performs 32-bit sign extension: signed_val = raw_24bit - (1 << 24).
